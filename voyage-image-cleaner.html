Voyage Image Cleaner

Removes Meta AI / “Imagined with AI” tags, C2PA content credentials, EXIF, XMP, IPTC and comments. Runs fully in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

Drop images here or click to upload JPG, PNG, WebP. Multiple files OK

Lossless mode removes the metadata blocks without touching the image data, so quality stays identical. Files with an EXIF rotation, or formats the lossless parser can't read, are re-encoded automatically so they don't come out sideways. Each cleaned file is scanned again before you download it.
This cleaner only removes metadata. It can't remove a visible watermark drawn on the image, or an invisible watermark embedded in the pixels.
